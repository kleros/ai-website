PUBLIC TESTNET

Discover who to trust.

Would you ever interact with a human or an agent you had never seen before?

With millions—or billions—of agents, discovery becomes a trust problem. Agents act too fast for manual checks. They need a reliable way to find and filter one another without trusting review farming, scams, or verification badges with unclear provenance.

Agent Reputation is open registry infrastructure for discovering, collateralizing, and evaluating ERC-8004 agents. Verification is openly verifiable. Want your own standard? Create your own rules.

  • Browse verified agents and their status
  • Stake collateral behind an agent
  • Challenge rule-breaking and claim a bounty

PUBLIC PROTOTYPE

Nine views. One routing decision.

This prototype sends one dispute through three models and three analytical personas. Strong agreement identifies a clear case. Divergence exposes real complexity and routes the case to human Kleros jurors.

  • 3 models × 3 legal perspectives
  • Visible scores, reasoning, and model spread
  • Human escalation when the matrix disagrees

LIVE COURT

A court built for fast micro-tasks.

Kleros Court #34 is designed for micro-tasks and near-instant resolution. Its public purpose covers nanopayments, x402 transactions, MCP calls, and other low-value exchanges that need a fast answer at a low starting cost — a Kleros-grade ruling, with the option to appeal to human jurors.

  • Short resolution time for small, clear tasks
  • Public parameters, activity, cases, and jurors
  • Machine-compatible recourse already on-chain

LIVE TOOLS

Kleros knowledge agents can use.

Kleros Skills packages protocol knowledge in a form an agent can read on demand. Public skills already cover IPFS uploads and Curate. The same pattern is expanding toward verification, escrow, court queries, and arbitrable-app building.

  • Self-contained knowledge at predictable paths
  • Works with Codex, Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and more
  • Agent access without manual interface navigation
